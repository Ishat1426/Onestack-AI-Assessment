# Decisions and ambiguities

1. **Default date:** Use the assignment's default, 2026-08-15, when no date is supplied.
2. **Circular effective date:** Treat circular clauses A, B, C, and E as active from 2026-08-01, per its heading.
3. **Refund amendment:** Treat deletion of master clause 3.3 as effective only from 2026-09-01. Before that date, the 24-hour refund exception remains; on/after that date, clause 3.2 applies without exception.
4. **MDR cap:** The circular sets the cap to ₹15 for monthly volume up to ₹10 lakh and ₹25 above ₹10 lakh. GST is outside the cap because master clause 2.4 says so.
5. **Daily limit:** Apply the circular's lower-of rule: ₹1,00,000 or the customer's own bank limit, whichever is lower. It is an aggregate daily account limit, not a per-transaction limit.
6. **Special merchant transaction limits:** The ₹2,00,000 per-transaction exception requires Capital Markets or Insurance category and enhanced KYC; the lower daily/bank limit can still constrain payment.
7. **Account closure wording:** Clause 5.2 says pending settlements are released within 10 working days of closure, after dues. The question's “closed yesterday” does not establish when the required 30-day written notice began or whether closure was processed, so don't promise a specific calendar date.
8. **Card same-day question:** Clause 1.2 says T+2; it does not explicitly discuss same-day settlement as a permission, so answer that the policy doesn't establish same-day settlement and cite 1.2.
9. **FAQ reliability:** The March FAQ is older than the August circular and conflicts with the master/circular on rates and caps. Treat it as evidence of what FAQ says, not as controlling policy where it conflicts.
10. **Status convention:** Use `CONFLICT_RESOLVED` when a dated amendment or authoritative master clause resolves conflicting material; use `CANNOT_ANSWER` when the sources don't support the requested detail.
11. **Arithmetic:** Calculate raw MDR, apply the cap to MDR only, calculate GST as 18% of capped MDR, then add GST.
12. **No invented citations:** Only cite clause numbers present in the provided documents. FAQ citations use the named FAQ question because FAQ entries have no numeric clause numbers.
